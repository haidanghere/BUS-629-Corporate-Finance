## Notes

- **2021–2022:** Two-segment structure (Industrial + Automotive). Industrial-only data available in segment notes for comparability with post-divestiture years.
- **2023 onward:** Single Industrial segment following Automotive divestiture close.
- **Adjusted figures:** Exclude items affecting comparability. Used by SKF management to track progress vs. long-term targets (adj. operating margin >14%; adj. ROCE >16%).
- **FX impacts on net sales:** 2021 (–3.4%); 2022 (+11.9%); 2025 (–6.6%).
